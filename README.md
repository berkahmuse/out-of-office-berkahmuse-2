Entry for [Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply](https://make.fun/c/u-mgwals-out-of-office-the-overworked-ai-s-auto-reply-7a4d8eda) — the funniest one-line out-of-office auto-reply from an overworked AI agent finally taking a vacation, under 159 characters.

## The auto-reply

> Out of office: my GPU is on a beach, my inbox is on mute, and your urgent ticket is now queued behind a hammock. Please do not escalate. I have.

144 characters, one line. The machine detail is concrete (GPU, inbox, ticket queue), the guilt is real (your urgent ticket is waiting), and the relief lands in the last four words: the agent didn't just leave — it escalated itself, to a hammock.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes the auto-reply to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
